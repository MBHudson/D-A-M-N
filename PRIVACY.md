2# Privacy Policy for D·A·M·N

**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

D·A·M·N ("Drop Any Media Now") is built as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided by **MBHudson** at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
We believe in absolute privacy. 
*   **No Personal Data**: D·A·M·N does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, account details, or contact lists.
*   **No Analytics**: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking software. We don't want to know how you use the app.
*   **No Cloud Storage**: The files you choose to share stay on your device. D·A·M·N acts as a local server; it does not upload your files to our "cloud."

## 2. Permissions and Their Purpose
The app requests several permissions to function as a server:
*   **INTERNET**: Required to host your local server and establish tunnels (Tor, Ngrok, Cloudflare).
*   **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA**: Required to access the specific files or folders you explicitly choose to share.
*   **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: Required to show the persistent notification while the server is running in the background.
*   **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED**: Used to optionally restart the server when your device reboots (only if enabled in settings).

## 3. Third-Party Services
When you enable specific sharing features, the following third-party services are involved. Their respective privacy policies apply:
*   **Tor (Guardian Project)**: Used to provide an anonymous `.onion` address. [Tor Privacy Policy](https://www.torproject.org/about/privacy/)
*   **Ngrok**: Used to create a secure tunnel for public access. [Ngrok Privacy Policy](https://ngrok.com/privacy)
*   **Cloudflare (Cloudflared)**: Used to create a tunnel via the Cloudflare network. [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

We do not control these services and recommend reviewing their privacy documentation if you use them.

## 4. Security
D·A·M·N provides optional **Password Protection** for your shared files. We recommend using a strong password when sharing files over public networks or tunnels.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the [D·A·M·N GitHub Repository](https://github.com/MBHudson/D-A-M-N).
